# OT review, IEC 62443 at a high level

Amberkloof's only production OT plant is the water and dust circuit at the fictional Red Dune Pit. This note gives you a structure. It does not assign security levels, gaps, or scores.

**Fictional exercise.** This is not an IEC 62443 certification, a zone design you could build from, or a copy of the standard. IEC 62443 is a paid family of standards. The names below are short themes so you can talk about them in an interview and in the report. If you need an official requirement sentence, read a licensed copy and do not paste it here.

Use the company profile for the asset list and the Purdue sketch. Confirm the sketch in the artisan and superintendent interviews before you treat a zone as real.

## How this family is used here

People usually point at these parts. You are not being asked to audit each part.

| Part | Plain use in this exercise |
| --- | --- |
| IEC 62443-2-1 | Whether there is a security programme that includes the plant, not only the office |
| IEC 62443-3-2 | How you group assets into zones and conduits, and how you think about risk |
| IEC 62443-3-3 | System requirements you might later map to a zone. Out of scope as a clause by clause review |
| IEC 62443-4-1 and 62443-4-2 | How a product supplier builds and hardens a component. Relevant when you talk to vendors, not as a product test |

A zone is a group of assets that share similar security needs. A conduit is a path between zones that you intend to control. The Purdue levels in the profile are a way to draw the groups. IEC 62443 zones do not have to match Purdue one for one. Say how you grouped them.

Security levels run from 1 to 4. A higher number means you want the zone to resist a more capable attacker. Pick a target only when you can say why the consequence deserves it. Leave the cell blank if you cannot. Do not label a zone as certified.

## What to write in the report

One short section is enough. Name the zones, the target levels you are willing to defend, the themes that worry you, and the evidence ids. Say that you did not test the plant and you did not certify it.

If a theme is already covered by an Annex A row or a CSF subcategory, point to that row. Do not maintain two silent opinions.
